Privacy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Who this is

The Coffee Numbers is run by Chris Williams. You can reach me at chris@thecoffeenumbers.com.

What the site collects

If you say yes to analytics, Google Analytics records which pages you visit, how long you stay on each one, which buttons you click, your device and browser type, your approximate location, and how you got here (for example, from Instagram). It doesn't collect your name or email address. If you say no, nothing is sent to Google Analytics.

Your answers in the tools

The health check start and the free calculators work in your browser. Your answers aren't sent to me unless you choose to email them.

If you email me

I'll use the details you send to reply to you and to talk through your numbers. I won't share them with anyone else.

If you register your interest

If you leave your email address on the coming soon page, I'll use it only to tell you when that area opens.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies (their names start with _ga) only if you accept. They can stay on your device for up to two years. The analytics data itself is kept for 14 months. I've turned off Google's advertising features, so the data isn't used to show you adverts.

Your choices

You can change your mind at any time. If you'd like me to delete anything you've emailed me, just ask.
